Privacy Policy

Eric WHOOP Analytics

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Eric WHOOP Analytics is a personal-use application intended to help its developer review personal fitness, recovery, sleep, and training information from WHOOP. This policy describes the intended handling of data if the application is connected to a WHOOP account.

1. Information Accessed

With your authorization, the application may access recovery information (including recovery score, heart rate variability and resting heart rate), sleep information, physiological cycles and strain, and workout information. Access is limited to permissions you approve in the WHOOP authorization process.

2. Purpose of Use

The information is used only for personal health, fitness, and training trend analysis, including planning and reviewing golf practice and recovery. This application is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnoses.

3. Storage and Sharing

During initial local testing, data may be processed and stored on the developer's own device. Any future use of third-party hosting, automation, or AI services will be assessed and this policy will be updated before data is sent to those services. Data is not sold. The application is not intended for public use by other WHOOP members.

4. Authorization and Revocation

You may revoke the application's access to your WHOOP data through WHOOP's connected-app controls. Revoking access prevents future authorized data retrieval, but does not automatically delete copies already stored locally. Local copies can be removed by the developer.

5. Security

The developer intends to limit access to authorized personal use and take reasonable steps to protect tokens and data. No electronic system can be guaranteed fully secure.

6. Policy Changes

This policy may change if the application's functionality, hosting, or data practices change. The date above will be updated when changes are made.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the contact email associated with the Eric WHOOP Analytics application on the WHOOP Developer Platform.
